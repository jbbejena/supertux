How to play

You control Player 2; the host controls Player 1, pauses and saves. Both players share the camera. Landscape gives more room. Keyboard: arrows/WASD, Space to jump, Ctrl for action.

Keep your invitation private. After a disconnect, ask the host to return to title and send a fresh invitation. Invitations last 15 minutes. Once both players join, rooms last up to an hour; waiting or paused rooms close after 10 minutes.
